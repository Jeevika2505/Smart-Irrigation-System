Operational flow

From reservoir data to field-level action.

The infrastructure combines crop science, network routing, and irrigation policy into a single system that helps manage scarce water resources responsibly.

  • 1
    Collect field data

    Capture crop type, farmer details, and moisture conditions.

  • 2
    Prioritize requests

    Rank routine and drought-relief requests by urgency and crop priority.

  • 3
    Deliver water smartly

    Monitor reachability and allocate limited water with clear records.

Allocation rules

Transparent rules for every water request.

1 → 2 → 3

High, medium, and low crop priorities are handled in that order.

Dry first

When priorities match, the lower soil-moisture field is processed first.

Reachable

Water is allocated only when working canals connect the reservoir to a field.

Partial

Available water can be allocated while the remainder stays pending.
