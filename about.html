About AgriFlow

Smart irrigation starts with data, strategy, and trust.

AgriFlow was built to bring clarity to irrigation planning in regions where water is scarce, crop demand varies, and network reliability matters. It helps teams decide when to irrigate, how much water to distribute, and which fields need priority support.

🌱

Crop-first approach

Every irrigation decision is shaped by crop priority, soil moisture level, and the urgency of the field request.

🛰️

Connected infrastructure

The platform maps every field and canal link to identify which land remains reachable from the reservoir.

🧠

Decisions that scale

By centralizing field history and request states, administrators can manage system-wide allocation in real time.

Mission

Sustainable water use for the next generation of farming.

We believe efficient water management is essential for dependable harvests and resilient food systems. Our system supports administrators, farmers, and field networks with a structure that is intuitive, dependable, and scalable.
